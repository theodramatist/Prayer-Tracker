Prayer Tracker

A daily prayer list: the Anglican Cycle of Prayer, the people of your church, your own prayer requests, and the Collect of the Day from the Book of Common Prayer, in Rite One or Rite Two.

The address

theodramatist.github.io/Prayer-Tracker/everyone/ Open Prayer Tracker

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the address above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Prayers from your Home Screen. It works like any other app, even with no connection.

On an Android phone

  1. Open the address above in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Prayers from your Home screen or app drawer.

Getting started

  • On the first screen, type your church’s name, then add its people. Each comes up on a day of the week (or everyone every day, if you prefer).
  • Tap + to add a prayer request, for a day, a week, a month, or until you take it off.
  • Tap the circle beside a name once you’ve prayed, or use Pray through the list to go one at a time.
  • In Settings, choose Rite One (traditional) or Rite Two (contemporary) language, and pick prayers from the Prayer Book’s Prayers and Thanksgivings.

Your list stays yours

  • It’s free, with no account and no sign-in.
  • Everything you add stays on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Because it’s kept only on your phone, save a backup now and then (Settings → Backup), and before you change phones. Removing the app, or clearing Safari’s website data, deletes your list.
  • New versions arrive by themselves.

The prayers and collects are from the Book of Common Prayer (1979), which is in the public domain. The Anglican Cycle of Prayer is © the Anglican Consultative Council and is shared free of charge under its terms.
